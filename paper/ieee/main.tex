\begin{abstract}
We determine the exact joint distribution of the code dimension and the
$k$-Galois hull dimension over all repeated-root constacyclic codes of a fixed
length over a square-free affine algebra over a finite field. With length
$n=n'p^{t}$ and component moduli that are $p^{t}$-th powers of square-free
kernels, a code is encoded by one exponent at each distinct kernel factor, and
the $k$-Galois dual acts on those factors through a Frobenius-twisted
reciprocal permutation. On each orbit of this permutation the hull dimension is
a cyclic sum of pairwise minima of consecutive exponents, so its bivariate law
is the trace of a power of an explicit weighted transfer matrix with
$p^{t}+1$ states; multiplying over orbits, with a dimension-only factor for
components whose twist is incompatible, gives a bivariate polynomial whose
coefficients are the exact multiplicities of the pairs (code dimension, hull
dimension). We obtain the marginal laws, the attainable pairs, closed
means and variances, conditional laws, and the numbers of LCD,
self-orthogonal, dual-containing and self-dual codes, and we give an algorithm
that is polynomial in the length, the multiplicity and the number of kernel
factors, checked against finite-field computations in characteristics
$2$, $3$ and $5$.
\end{abstract}
