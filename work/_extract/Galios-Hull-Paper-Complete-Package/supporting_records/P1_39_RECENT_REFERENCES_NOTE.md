## Seven references added (all metadata checked against publisher/DOI records)

1. **ACLMRS2024** — Anderson, Camps-Moreno, López, Matthews, Ruano,
   Soprunov, *Relative hulls and quantum codes*, IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory
   70(5) (2024), 3190–3201, doi:10.1109/TIT.2024.3373550.
   *Cited:* Introduction, Galois-hull literature paragraph (after the
   DIMP2026 clause). *Supports:* relative hulls for general linear codes
   include the \(e\)-Galois case and have quantum-code consequences.
2. **DS2024** — Dougherty & Şahinkaya, *On cyclic and negacyclic codes
   with one-dimensional hulls and their applications*, Adv. Math. Commun.
   18(5) (2024), 1364–1378, doi:10.3934/amc.2022096.
   *Cited:* same paragraph, after the Talbi2021 clause. *Supports:*
   existence/characterization of cyclic and negacyclic codes with
   one-dimensional hulls.
3. **DPDC2025** — Debnath & Prakash, *Hulls of separable double cyclic
   codes over \(\mathbb{Z}_{p^2}\)*, Comput. Appl. Math. 44 (2025),
   Article 255, doi:10.1007/s40314-025-03206-x.
   *Cited:* same paragraph, after the Talbi2021 clause. *Supports:* hull
   \(p\)-dimensions, average \(p\)-dimensions, and counts at fixed hull
   \(p\)-dimension for separable double cyclic codes over
   \(\mathbb{Z}_{p^2}\). (The publisher article number is 255; an AIMS
   citing page's "41" is not used.)
4. **LS2024** — Li & Shi, *Characterization and classification of binary
   linear codes with various hull dimensions from an improved mass
   formula*, IEEE Trans. Inf. Theory 70(5) (2024), 3357–3372,
   doi:10.1109/TIT.2023.3298379.
   *Cited:* same paragraph, after the DIYP2026 sentence. *Supports:* the
   binary case of counting linear codes by hull dimension via a mass
   formula.
5. **LSL2025** — Li, Shi & Ling, *A mass formula for linear codes with
   prescribed hull dimension and related classification*, IEEE Trans.
   Inf. Theory 71(1) (2025), 273–286, doi:10.1109/TIT.2024.3425058.
   *Cited:* same location as LS2024. *Supports:* mass formulas counting
   general linear codes by prescribed hull dimension.
6. **BBO2026** — Bouyuklieva, Bouyukliev & Özbudak, *Sequence of numbers
   of linear codes with increasing hull dimensions*, Des. Codes Cryptogr.
   94 (2026), Article 39, doi:10.1007/s10623-025-01776-9.
   *Cited:* same location as LS2024. *Supports:* for fixed \((n,k)\), the
   number of linear codes decreases as the hull dimension increases.
7. **LYW2025** — Liu, Yu & Wang, *Several constructions of linear codes
   with various Galois hull dimensions*, Comput. Appl. Math. 44 (2025),
   Article 299, doi:10.1007/s40314-025-03261-4.
   *Cited:* same location as LS2024. *Supports:* constructions of linear
   codes with prescribed/determinable Galois hull dimensions from a given
   code.
