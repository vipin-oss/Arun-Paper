## Referee-level remarks

1. **The hypotheses are load-bearing and are stated as such.** The
   compatibility condition `lambda^{1+p^{d-k}} = 1` is used exactly where the
   paper says it is (same-modulus dual), the incompatible case is explicitly
   quarantined, and the `P = 1` reduction is given its own proposition.
2. **The `nu_P` term is a real distinction, not cosmetics.** Length-one orbits
   have hull mean `nu_P` while longer orbits have edge mean `mu_P`; the paper
   makes this the pedagogical point of Example 9.3, and the numbers check out.
3. **The orientation remark is correct and useful.** `min(P-a,b) - min(P-b,a) =
   b - a` telescopes, so no statistic depends on the direction of the orbit;
   the predecessor convention matters only for identifying the hull generator.
   This prevents a whole class of referee objections.
4. **No gap was found**, and no theorem was modified. The two changes to the
   manuscript are terminology/claim-strength only.

## Recommendation

Accept in substance, minor revision in form. The only remaining scientific
debt is that Examples 9.4–9.6 were not independently reproduced here.
